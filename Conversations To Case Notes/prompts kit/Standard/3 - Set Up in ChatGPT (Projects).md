# Set Up in ChatGPT — Projects

**Time:** about ten minutes, once. **You need:** access to your organization's ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu, or Healthcare workspace.

---

## Before you start

Confirm you're in **ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu, or ChatGPT for Healthcare** — or using the OpenAI API.

**ChatGPT Free, Plus, Pro, and Business cannot be covered.** OpenAI states it directly: they don't offer a BAA for ChatGPT Business. That one surprises people, because Business sounds like the business plan. It isn't the covered one.

---

## The steps

1. Sign in to your organization's **ChatGPT Enterprise / Edu / Healthcare** workspace.
2. In the left sidebar, click **+ → Project**. Name it **Case Documentation Assistant**.
3. Open the project's **Instructions**.
4. Paste the **entire** Master Instructions block from *Starter / 2 - Master Instructions*.
5. **Save.**
6. *Optional:* add *Starter / 8 - Case Note Format Templates* to the project's **files**.

---

## Using it day to day

1. Start a chat **inside the project**.
2. Paste the client conversation.
3. Paste one task prompt from the Starter folder.
4. Read the Review & Sign-off block, correct, and sign.

---

## Test it

New chat inside the project. Paste a few sentences. Ask for a case-file summary.

**Working:** sections, neutral tone, review block, no instructions pasted.
**Not working:** the instructions didn't save, or you started the chat outside the project.

---

## One thing to avoid

ChatGPT also lets you put standing instructions in **Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions**, which applies them to your whole account.

**Use the project instead.** Account-wide custom instructions bleed into *every* conversation you have — including ones that have nothing to do with casework. You'll find the AI writing your emails in flat clinical case-note prose and refusing to speculate about anything. Keep the documentation behavior scoped to the place you do documentation.

---

## Keeping it clean

**Build it in the covered workspace only.** Not a personal account.

**Check the project's sharing.** If the project is shared, others can see the conversations in it. Client conversations included.

**Uploaded files live in the workspace.** Templates are fine. Real client files are protected information and belong only where they're permitted.
